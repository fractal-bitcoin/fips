# FIP\-30\_25\-26\_Retroactive\_Development\_Rewards\_Draft

**FIP:** 30  

**Author:** Fractal Team  

**Status:** Draft  

**Type:** Process  

**Created:** 2026\-09\-08



## Abstract

FIP\-30 proposes a one\-time Fractal 2025–2026 Retroactive Development Rewards Program to identify and reward products and technical tools that developers or teams independently completed before the launch of the program, that are already live or practically usable, and that have generated verifiable value for Fractal users, developers, or infrastructure\.

This program rewards completed historical contributions\. It is not intended to fund future development plans, does not require award recipients to commit to new development milestones, and does not calculate rewards directly based on reported development costs, team size, or development time\. The program will select no more than 10 projects, with a reference reward of 1,000–3,000 FB per project\. Depending on each project's actual needs, the Fractal Team may also provide other support, such as API credits, official promotion, or inclusion in project directories\.

Fractal will confirm and announce candidate projects after initial review, evidence verification, and communication with project teams, followed by a seven\-day public period for community feedback and additional nominations\. The Fractal Team will give full consideration to community input\. The Fractal Team will determine the final award recipients, reward amounts, and forms of support through a comprehensive review, and will publicly announce the results to the community\.



---



## Proposal Overview

- **Reward completed contributions\.** Eligible projects must already be live before the review, with validated product logic and an active community and user base, rather than consisting only of a concept, demo, or future roadmap\.

- **Define a clear scope of eligibility\.** Contributions must have a clear connection to Fractal and must have been completed, launched, or had their principal impact between January 1, 2025, and the nomination deadline\.

- **Use evidence\-based evaluation\.** Reviews will focus on completion, ecosystem value, actual impact, product quality, and prior support, while verifying project attribution, usage data, and previous funding\.

- **Provide one\-time rewards\.** The indicative reward is 1,000–3,000 FB per project and may be combined with appropriate other ecosystem support\.

- **Combine community discovery with team oversight\. **The community may submit nominations, provide feedback, and contribute additional evidence, while the Fractal Team is responsible for final verification, review, publication, and distribution\.



## Motivation

### A Reward Mechanism Based on Actual Results

Retroactive rewards assess results first, then evaluate them based on completion, actual usage, and ecosystem value\. Because the subjects under review already exist as usable or verifiable products, assessments can rely more on facts and outcomes than on development plans, cost estimates, or future commitments\.

FIP\-30 adopts this mechanism to provide one\-time recognition and rewards for completed contributions that created verifiable value for the Fractal ecosystem during a specified contribution period\.



### Recognizing Independently Completed Ecosystem Contributions

After identifying user needs, technical issues, or infrastructure gaps, some developers independently build dashboards, wallets, data tools, developer tools, monitoring systems, and other products\.

These builders assess needs on their own, invest their time, and assume the risks of early\-stage development\. Their contributions to ecosystem development should not be overlooked\.

Through retroactive rewards, Fractal can verify the completion status, practical role, and ecosystem value of these contributions after results have materialized, and publicly recognize eligible contributions\.



### Discovering, Documenting, and Showcasing Ecosystem Achievements

Products and tools in the Fractal ecosystem may be developed separately by individual developers, independent teams, companies, or community organizations, with information scattered across websites, code repositories, social channels, and on\-chain records\. Some valuable projects may remain under\-discovered because they lack stable channels for visibility\.

Public nominations, candidate announcements, and community feedback can help Fractal identify overlooked projects, supplement evidence of usage, correct errors in project ownership or data, and create an accessible record of project names, primary contributions, and reasons for receiving rewards\.

This process not only helps determine reward recipients, but also increases the visibility of high\-quality tools and builders, making it easier for users and developers to understand the products and infrastructure capabilities already available in the Fractal ecosystem\.



### Defining Reward Boundaries and Improving Resource Efficiency

Retroactive rewards should recognize completed contributions that have not already received materially equivalent support for the same deliverables\. The review must verify project attribution, actual contributions, and relevant prior support to prevent the same deliverables from being rewarded twice under different arrangements\. Receiving a reward means only that the Fractal team recognizes the project's verifiable contribution during a specified historical period; it does not constitute an investment, security audit, commercial partnership, or commitment to long\-term operations\.

This proposal independently defines the current retroactive developer rewards\. It neither assumes that Fractal has launched or will launch other developer funding programs, nor commits Fractal to any future funding arrangement\.

## Scope and Eligibility

### Eligible Contribution Period and Nominees

This reward applies to contributions that were substantially completed and launched, and that generated their primary impact on the Fractal ecosystem, between January 1, 2025, and the nomination deadline\. A project may have started before January 1, 2025, but its core deliverables must have been completed or launched during the applicable contribution period, or generated their primary impact during that period\.

Individual developers, informal teams, companies, community organizations, and other entities able to demonstrate attribution of the contribution are eligible for nomination\. Nominations may be submitted by project teams themselves or initiated by community members\. For a community nomination, the Fractal team should contact the project team to confirm the relevant information before the final review\.

For projects completed by multiple contributors, the project team should confirm its representative, principal contributors, attribution of contributions, and payment arrangements\. The Fractal team will evaluate and distribute rewards according to the information ultimately confirmed by the project team and is not responsible for adjudicating internal disputes over ownership or reward allocation\.



### Connection to the Fractal Network

A candidate project must have at least one of the following connections:

- It operates directly on the Fractal network or supports Fractal assets, transactions, nodes, miners, indexing, or staking activities;

- It provides Fractal users or developers with clear product capabilities, data capabilities, or improvements in operational efficiency;

- It makes a substantive Fractal adaptation of a general\-purpose open\-source component and can demonstrate that the adaptation has been used or created value;

- It improves the accessibility, reliability, transparency, or developer experience of the Fractal ecosystem in another verifiable way\.



### Eligible Project Types

Eligible deliverables include, but are not limited to:

- Dashboards for ecosystem data, indexing, staking, or network\-operations monitoring;

- Tools for assets, transactions, blocks, nodes, or network status;

- Wallets, SDKs, APIs, code libraries, debugging tools, and other developer tools;

- Automated monitoring, alerting, data\-analysis, and operations tools;

- Other completed products that improve the experience of Fractal users or developers\.



### Completion Requirements

Candidate projects must already meet the following basic requirements:

- The project is live and usable, and its core deliverables can be independently verified by reviewers;

- The nominee can provide product links, code, deployment records, on\-chain records, screenshots, documentation, or other materials sufficient to demonstrate completion;

- The nominee can explain the attribution of the deliverables and its specific contribution to them;

- There is no significant dispute concerning security, integrity, or attribution that would make a reward inappropriate\.



---



## Evaluation Framework

### Core Evaluation Criteria

- **Completion:** Whether the product is live, usable, reproducible, or verifiable, and whether its core functions are sufficiently complete for practical use\.

- **Ecosystem value:** Whether the deliverable solves real problems for Fractal users, developers, miners, node operators, or infrastructure participants\.

- **Actual impact:** Whether there is evidence of real users, transactions, calls, visits, integrations, on\-chain activity, community adoption, or other verifiable impact\.

- **Product quality:** Whether the deliverable demonstrates originality, stability, maintainability, enduring value, and sufficient Fractal\-specific adaptation\.

- **Historical support:** Whether the same deliverable, or any materially equivalent part of it, has previously received development fees, funding, or other equivalent resource support from Fractal or related entities\.



### Evidence and Verification

The review may consider one or more of the following types of evidence:

- An accessible product, test environment, code repository, release record, or documentation;

- On\-chain transactions, contracts, addresses, nodes, block production, calls, or other verifiable records;

- Deduplicated user, traffic, transaction, API call, or activity data, together with an explanation of the measurement methodology;

- Third\-party integrations, community usage, public feedback, citations, or project listings;

- Information on project attribution, division of responsibilities, contribution timelines, and prior funding;

- Other materials that the Fractal Team considers helpful for verifying the contribution\.



### Evaluation Principles

The criteria above are intended to support a holistic assessment and do not constitute a mechanical scoring formula\. Reward amounts will not be calculated in proportion to development costs, team size, development time, or similar factors\.

The review should prioritize ecosystem value that has already been generated and can be verified, while also comparing project maturity, scope of impact, enduring value, and historical support\. Where data cannot be fully disclosed, the project team may provide the Fractal Team with a reasonable method of verification, but unverifiable claims should not serve as a primary basis for a reward\.

## Rewards and Support

### FB Reward Range

As a general principle, rewards for individual projects will range from 1,000 to 3,000 FB\.

This program does not establish fixed reward tiers\. The Fractal Team will determine the specific reward amount through a comprehensive assessment of the project's completion, ecosystem value, actual impact, product quality, scope of contribution, and any funding or resource support previously received for the same work\.

Projects that meet the basic eligibility criteria, are complete, and can demonstrate clear value to the Fractal ecosystem may receive a reward of at least 1,000 FB\. Projects that have achieved broader use, solved important ecosystem problems, provided critical infrastructure capabilities, or delivered significant lasting value may receive a larger reward, capped at 3,000 FB per project\.

This retroactive reward is intended to reward no more than 10 projects\. The actual number of recipients and the total reward amount may be adjusted based on the quality of candidate projects, verification results, and the budget available\.



### Additional Ecosystem Support

In addition to FB rewards, the Fractal Team may provide one or more of the following forms of support based on each project’s needs:

- API or infrastructure credits and related usage discounts;

- Promotion through Fractal’s official channels;

- Inclusion in relevant ecosystem directories, documentation, or developer resources;

- Other ecosystem resources that may support the project’s continued development\.

Awards may consist of FB rewards, additional ecosystem support, or a combination of both\. The availability and terms of such support will depend on the Fractal Team’s available resources and the specific needs of each project\.



### One\-Time Distribution

As a general principle, FB rewards under this program will be distributed as a one\-time payment after the final results are announced, without installment milestones tied to future development progress\. Before receiving payment, the project team should confirm the recipient entity, payment address, and any other necessary information reasonably requested by the Fractal Team\.



---



## Ineligibility Criteria

A project should not receive a reward if any of the following applies:

- The product is unfinished and consists only of a concept, design mockup, demo, test fragment, or future development plan;

- The project has no clear connection to Fractal, or its actual value to Fractal users, developers, or the ecosystem cannot be explained;

- The product's status, impact data, project attribution, or the nominee's actual contribution cannot be verified;

- The same deliverables have already received development fees, funding, or other equivalent resource support from Fractal or related entities;

- The project substantially copies another project without creating a meaningful Fractal adaptation or additional value;

- The project provides false, misleading, or unreasonably inexplicable user, transaction, usage, or community data;

- There are serious security risks, fraud, malicious conduct, major attribution disputes, or conduct that misleads users;

- The Fractal team determines that a reward would be inappropriate because of compliance, integrity, or other material risks\.

If only part of a project has previously received financial or in\-kind support from Fractal or related entities, the review may consider only new contributions that can be clearly distinguished, were completed independently, and have not already been supported\. If the contributions cannot reasonably be separated, no duplicate reward will be granted\.

## Nomination, Review, and Distribution Process

### Process Overview

| Stage | Timing | Tasks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Initial Review | Before public announcement | Identify candidate projects and prepare preliminary eligibility and reward recommendations. |
| Candidate Announcement and Community Feedback | Days 1–7 | Publish the candidate list and invite community feedback, factual corrections, and additional nominations. |
| Verification and Outreach | Days 8–14 | Verify project ownership, actual contributions, prior funding, supporting evidence, and the project team’s willingness to participate. |
| Final Review | Days 15–21 | Verify new information and determine award recipients, reward amounts, and forms of support. |
| Final Announcement and Distribution | Day 21 onward | Publish the final results and distribute rewards in a one-time payment after verifying payment details. |

### Initial Review

Based on publicly available information and its observations of the ecosystem, the Fractal Team will identify potential candidate projects and conduct an initial review in accordance with the scope and evaluation criteria set out in this proposal\. The initial review is intended to facilitate efficient verification and does not indicate that a project will receive an award\.



### Candidate Announcement and Community Feedback

Once the candidates that pass initial review are publicly announced, a seven\-day feedback period will begin\. The community may submit additional nominations, provide evidence of usage, identify errors in data or ownership, disclose prior funding, or present other facts relevant to eligibility and reward determinations\.

Feedback should include verifiable supporting materials wherever possible\. The number of endorsements, likes, or level of public attention alone will not serve as an automatic basis for receiving a reward\.

The candidate announcement means only that a project has entered the review process\. It does not constitute a promise of a reward, nor does it represent the Fractal Team's endorsement of the project's security, investment value, or long\-term operations\.



### Verification and Outreach with Project Teams

For projects included in the candidate list or nominated by the community, the Fractal Team will use reasonable efforts to contact the project team and confirm project ownership, primary contributions, product status, historical funding, and information that may be publicly disclosed\.

Before the final results are announced, the award recipient and payment arrangements should also be confirmed to reduce the risk of errors regarding project ownership or payment information\. Projects for which contact cannot be established, information cannot be confirmed, or material disputes exist will be disqualified\.



### Final Review, Announcement, and Distribution

After the feedback period ends, the Fractal Team will verify material feedback and determine the final award recipients, reward amounts, and forms of support through a comprehensive assessment based on the evaluation criteria in this proposal\.

The final announcement should include, at a minimum, each project's name, primary contribution, reason for receiving a reward, and form of reward\. Once the necessary identity, ownership, and payment information has been verified, FB rewards will generally be distributed as a one\-time payment\.

## Goals and Non\-Goals

### Goals

- Identify and reward completed Fractal ecosystem contributions that generated practical value between 2025 and 2026;

- Evaluate projects based primarily on completion, ecosystem value, actual impact, product quality, and verifiable evidence;

- Encourage developers to independently build products and technical tools that address real problems within Fractal;

- Use public candidate announcements and community feedback to discover overlooked contributions, correct facts, and improve transparency;

- Prevent the same deliverable from receiving materially equivalent duplicate support or rewards through different arrangements\.



### Non\-Goals

FIP\-30 is not intended to:

- Provide advance funding for unfinished products, concepts, or future development plans;

- Replace or restrict other developer incentives that Fractal may establish independently in the future;

- Establish a formula that automatically calculates rewards based on costs, hours worked, team size, vote count, or social\-media attention;

- Guarantee any project ongoing funding, a commercial partnership, investment, user growth, liquidity, or market performance;

- Provide a security audit, proof of vulnerability\-free status, investment advice, or long\-term official endorsement for rewarded projects;

- Create a permanent, automatically renewing, or mandatory annual reward obligation\.



---



## Conclusion

FIP\-30 proposes a one\-time Fractal 2025–2026 Retroactive Development Rewards for completed, usable products and technical tools that have generated practical value\. The program focuses on results and evidence rather than future commitments, development costs, or team size\.

This program is expected to select no more than 10 projects, with a reference reward of 1,000–3,000 FB per project, which may be combined with appropriate non\-cash support\. Candidate projects will undergo internal screening, evidence verification, and confirmation with project teams, followed by a seven\-day public period for community feedback and additional nominations\.

After verifying the feedback, the Fractal Team will make the final decisions, announce each project's name, primary contribution, reason for receiving a reward, and form of reward, and complete a one\-time distribution\.

By defining clear boundaries for eligibility, evaluation, exclusion, transparency, and non\-endorsement, this proposal aims to recognize historical contributions in a controlled, verifiable manner without duplicate funding, while increasing the visibility of high\-quality tools and builders in the Fractal ecosystem\.
